Use the osmossi-studio skill.

Plan Pinterest Pins for: $ARGUMENTS (if empty, the next 30 days).

1. Query `posts` for the period. List the Instagram posts and the Pins already planned.
2. Propose Pins that reuse those posts: an image Pin for each product post, a Video Pin for each Reel. Aim for 2–3 Pins per product over time. Skip any that already exist. Schedule them on Mon/Wed/Fri at 20:00, a day or two after the Instagram post, and earlier for gift content (Pins take weeks to gain reach).
3. Show the proposal as a table and ask for a yes.
4. On yes, create them in one `batch` with `status: "idea"` and a `notes` line saying which Instagram post each one reuses.
5. Confirm and give the app link.
